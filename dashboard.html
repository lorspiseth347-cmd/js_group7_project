Help Center

Answers to common questions.

How do I add a new car?

Open My Cars and click Add New Car, then fill in the model, type and daily rate.

How do I confirm a pending booking?

Go to Bookings, filter by Pending and review each reservation.

Where can I see my earnings?

The Payments page shows received, pending and refunded amounts with every transaction.
