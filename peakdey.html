Common Questions

Quick answers before you book.

What do I need to rent a car?

A valid driving license, your ID or passport, and a deposit. Foreign visitors need an international driving permit.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before pick-up.

Is fuel included?

Fuel is included only with driver packages. For self-drive, return the car with the same fuel level.

What if the car breaks down?

Call our 24/7 support line. We send help or a replacement car as fast as possible.
